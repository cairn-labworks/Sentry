OpenDash turns your Android phone into a capable dash cam. It records continuously in the background while you use navigation, music, and other apps without taking over the screen.

Recordings loop within a storage budget you set, so the app can run indefinitely without filling the phone. Important moments can be starred so they are never overwritten.

Features:

* Record fixed-length clips in the background using the rear camera.
* Automatically delete the oldest non-starred clips within a chosen storage limit.
* Star important clips so they survive automatic rotation.
* Burn date, time, GPS location, and speed into the video.
* Browse, filter, select, and delete recordings.
* View a live camera preview with a GPS and speed display.
* Control recording from a draggable overlay widget.
* Start recording from a home-screen shortcut.
* Choose the recording resolution, including UHD on supported devices.
* Apply night scene mode during a configurable schedule.
* Select system, light, or dark theme.
* Receive overheating warnings and optionally stop on low battery.
* Automatically start and stop recording when power is connected or disconnected.

Location is optional and is used only for the on-video location and speed overlay.
